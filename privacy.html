Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Data Lifecycle

1. Image Processing & Ephemeral Memory

Images you upload or capture via Camera Lens are transmitted securely to our server, which forwards them directly to Google Gemini via OpenRouter to perform code extraction.

  • In-Memory Processing: Images are processed strictly in volatile memory. They are never written to disk, saved to permanent databases, or retained once extraction finishes.
  • No Image Training: CodeSnapper does not retain or use your images or screenshots to train our own proprietary models.

Account & History

2. Account Data & Extraction History

Signed-in Users: When you register for a free CodeSnapper account, we store:

  • Your email address (stored in lowercase).
  • A securely salted and hashed password using bcrypt. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Your extracted code text, saved in your personal extraction history for 90 days so you can easily access previous snippets. You can rename or delete snippets at any time.

Anonymous Users: If you use CodeSnapper without signing in, no personal account information is collected. Your IP address is processed server-side solely to calculate and enforce the free extraction limit (25 total extractions without an account).

Analytics & Storage

3. Cookies & Usage Analytics

We use essential cookies to keep you signed into your account securely across page navigation.

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregate usage statistics (such as page views, visitor regions, and browser types) to understand how the service performs and where to improve it. No screenshots, images, or extracted code snippets are ever transmitted to Google Analytics.

Third Parties

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

CodeSnapper utilizes trusted third-party infrastructure to host the application, store account data, and perform visual code recognition:

  • Render: Cloud application platform providing hosting and server infrastructure (Render Privacy Policy).
  • Turso: Managed cloud database provider used to securely store user accounts, rate limit records, and 90-day extraction history (Turso Privacy Policy).
  • OpenRouter: Acts as our API gateway for model inference (OpenRouter Terms).
  • Google Gemini Vision: Provides multimodal vision processing (Google Gemini API Terms).

Please note that under free-tier AI API terms, third-party model providers may use submitted data in accordance with their respective privacy policies to refine and improve models.

Support

5. Feedback & Issue Reports

When you submit an issue or feature suggestion using our built-in Report an Issue modal, the report details (category and description) are stored in our secure database so our engineering team can review and resolve bugs.
